# 09 — Evidence policy: amendment + relevance

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Two reserved-to-V decisions from the reports:

1. May frozen search terms be amended during a run — and if so, can amendment
   results support CONFIRMATION, or exploration only?
2. Is wholly or partly off-subject evidence rejected outright, downgraded with
   a recorded reason, or governed by a mixed rule?

## Settles

Human decisions #1 and #2 (report-for-humans, "Decisions only V can make");
constrains the HARVEST and WEIGH stage rows in the spec.

## Answer

V ruled in sitting #1 (2026-08-03): **DR-008** — typed amendments: mechanical
repairs keep confirmation power, semantic re-aims are exploration-only until
re-frozen; all logged, visible at serving. **DR-009** — mixed relevance rule:
wholly off-subject rejected with logged reason, partly relevant admitted
downgraded with the share named. Full text in
[../decisions-ledger.md](../decisions-ledger.md).
