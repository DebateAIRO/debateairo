Findings:

1. **blocker — research-opus.md §(c).2–§(c).3 (hinge detection and overlay applicability).** Evidence: the packet defines a value hinge only when a single QBAF node has opposite-polarity children, `|v_s-v_a| <= epsilon`, divergent value tags, and zero abstentions. That is not a necessary condition for a value-decided choice. Two alternatives may each have decisive, non-balanced evidence on different criteria (one clearly cheaper, the other clearly safer) while the option ranking still depends entirely on value weights; Grok's E2 utility table is already a counterexample. Such a hinge may contain no locally balanced bipolar node at all. The proposed detector would silently serve an evidence ranking for a genuine values question. **Concrete fix:** define hinges over option-by-criterion outcome vectors: after the unjudged gate, test Pareto dominance and then compute whether the preferred option varies over the admissible value-weight domain (weight-stability regions). Use the local `|v_s-v_a|` test only as one heuristic signal, not as the definition. Specify behavior when utilities or criterion mappings abstain.

2. **major — research-opus.md §(a) F1–F3 / SPEC-C (the recommended minimal tau table is not yet computable).** Evidence: the claimed lookup key includes `verification_status`, `source_class`, `corroboration_bucket`, and categorical judge output, but the packet supplies neither exhaustive domains nor the mapping to tau, calibration procedure, conflict rule, or treatment of absent/contradictory keys. “One judge call, already paid” is a hidden oracle, not computation from graph+evidence alone; “all inputs exist” does not define their semantics. **Concrete fix:** either (i) mark the table explicitly as human-owned policy input rather than graph-derived computation, and specify its versioned schema, complete row selection, conflict/absence behavior, provenance, calibration and validation; or (ii) provide a deterministic algorithm over existing evidence fields. Keep missing rows behind the outgoing-edge abstention gate.

3. **major — research-opus.md THM-5 / F3 (probability language overclaims a theorem).** Evidence: `1-product(1-v_i)` has the same algebraic form as the probability of a union under independence, but a DF-QuAD t-conorm is not thereby a probabilistic model and does not prove that siblings are independent evidence events. Therefore “corroboration and redundancy are one knob” and “no room for a corroboration bonus” do not follow as theorems. They may still be prudent design constraints. **Concrete fix:** relabel this as a modelling interpretation/design rule; distinguish claim duplication, provenance dependence, and independent corroboration; then prove or empirically calibrate any rule that collapses families or changes tau so the same evidence is rewarded exactly once.

4. **major — research-opus.md F4 / SPEC greedy similarity adjustment (underspecified and pathologically gameable).** Evidence: “order siblings by adjusted strength” is circular before adjustment; the product over earlier “kept” nodes gives no definition of `kept`, similarity provenance, embedding/model/version, threshold/prefilter, or tie behavior beyond node id. It is order-sensitive in non-transitive similarity chains and can over-discount C because C resembles already-discounted B even when C is independent of A. At `k=50`, there are 1,225 pairs per family; the packet calls embeddings negligible without a latency/memory budget and leaves an unbounded number of entailment calls above its unspecified threshold. Local sibling-only comparison also permits duplicate laundering by inserting intermediate nodes. **Concrete fix:** give a fully deterministic clustering/adjustment algorithm, immutable similarity inputs and versions, transitivity policy, complexity and call cap at `k=50`, caching/invalidation, and an overflow fallback. Canonicalize provenance families across intermediate nodes and test star, chain, clique, adversarial-paraphrase, and duplicated-subgraph cases.

5. **major — research-opus.md THM-7 / §(c).4 (blast radius and cost are wrong for a DAG).** Evidence: a hinge can have multiple transitive-successor branches which later reconverge; affected nodes are the whole induced successor subgraph, not necessarily “the path from the hinge to the root.” Recomputing only one path can leave stale strengths. **Concrete fix:** state the affected set as all reachable successors of every modified edge endpoint and bound incremental recomputation by `O(|V_succ|+|E_succ|)` per branch (or the actual dependency-index cost), with tests for fan-out and reconvergence. Retain the valid invariant that non-successors are unchanged.

6. **major — research-opus.md F6 and E1 flip-sensitivity (general derivative specification is incomplete).** Evidence: the text gives the supporter product for both polarities. In a supporter-dominant region, `d sigma/d v_i` for a supporter contains the product of the *other supporters*, while for an attacker it contains the product of the *other attackers*; they are not the same sibling product. Reverse accumulation must also sum contributions along every path in a DAG. Similarity adjustment creates cross-derivatives and discrete reorder boundaries, while the packet discusses only the DF-QuAD kink. E1 happens to have one attacker, so its `-0.5` survives, but the advertised general algorithm does not. **Concrete fix:** specify piecewise Jacobians for attacker aggregation, supporter aggregation, adjustment, edge weighting, and mediation; reverse-accumulate over all outgoing paths; define nondifferentiable/reorder fallback to exact perturbation; state whether reported impact is with respect to original or adjusted strength.

7. **major — research-opus.md F3–F4 / §(g) (missing global gaming cases).** Evidence: acyclicity of argument edges does not prevent circular provenance (“A cites B, B cites A”), and sibling-only family collapse does not catch one source repeated beneath different intermediate claims. Both permit corroboration or weight laundering while passing the local rule. **Concrete fix:** maintain a provenance-lineage DAG separate from the argument DAG, reject or collapse lineage cycles, assign stable origin-family IDs transitively, and enforce duplicate budgets across the relevant ancestor cone rather than only immediate siblings. Add invariance tests showing that inserting identity intermediate nodes cannot increase root strength.

8. **blocker — research-grok.md F2 / abstention table / E1 (unjudged-node gate is hand-waved).** Evidence: the packet alternates among “beta undefined,” “excluded from phi aggregates,” “parent abstains,” and “shows interval,” but never places one exact gate in the propagation formula. It does not define mixed judged/unjudged children or a non-leaf unjudged node whose judged supporters could resurrect a positive strength. In E1, U is not even attached to a specified parent/polarity. This fails the named fabricated-confidence hard gate. **Concrete fix:** for point DF-QuAD, retain the node visibly but suppress every outgoing influence edge before attack/support aggregation (edge weight zero on the weighted path, edge omission on the unweighted path), regardless of the node's recursively computed strength; separately propagate the typed abstention metadata. Specify mixed, all-abstaining, and non-leaf cases. If intervals are offered, define them as a separate complete semantics rather than an “or.”

9. **blocker — research-grok.md E1 Step 3–5 (worked structural formula and arithmetic are not DF-QuAD).** Evidence: Grok uses `(phi+ - phi-)/(1-phi-)`. Canonical DF-QuAD uses `beta + (1-beta)(phi+ - phi-)` when support dominates, and `beta-beta(phi- - phi+)` otherwise. With `beta=0.5`, `phi+=0.955`, `phi-=0.60`, the correct result is `0.6775`, not `0.94375`. Removing the duplicate cluster gives `0.55`, not `~0.625`; removing A1 gives `0.9775`. The “or better beta=0” alternative is also a distinct, unexplained semantics choice. **Concrete fix:** replace the formula and all dependent arithmetic with canonical DF-QuAD, declare a provenance-bearing base score for R (or specify a separate pure decision-node aggregation semantics), and recompute the tornado ranking from exact removal runs.

10. **major — research-grok.md F4–F7 and minimal set (factor computations and costs are not specification-ready).** Evidence: redundancy is “max or soft-OR,” family identity is an open list of examples, an optional independence bonus has no formula, embeddings have no model/threshold/version, and judged quality comes from an unspecified human/agent oracle. “Embeddings; can cache” omits the required `O(k^2)` pair count (1,225 at `k=50`), cache invalidation, and model-call cap. Exact Shapley is merely called “high” rather than exponential; leave-one-out is not bounded (`O(k(|V|+|E|))` for k tested removals on a full DAG absent incremental machinery). **Concrete fix:** choose one duplicate aggregator, define family/lineage identity and missing-metadata behavior, delete or formulate/calibrate the bonus, identify external judgments as policy inputs, and provide time/space/call bounds and `k=50` fallback for every recommended factor.

11. **major — research-grok.md §(f) (verification labels exceed the evidence described).** Evidence: entries marked **VERIFIED** include “via ArgRAG + multiple secondary cites,” “secondary + ArgRAG,” “standard citation,” “secondary confirmations,” and “via ArgRAG citation chain.” Those are not primary-content verification, despite the packet's claim that statuses limit claims to accessible evidence. The primary ArgRAG record does verify deterministic QBAF reasoning and uniform `beta=0.5`; the primary Yin et al. arXiv record verifies EW-QBAFs and G-RAEs, but an abstract record alone does not verify all implementation claims or a KR 2026 extended-form attribution. **Concrete fix:** downgrade every secondary-chain item to PARTIAL/SECONDARY, separate metadata verification from content verification, attach a primary URL/DOI/arXiv ID to each load-bearing claim, and remove venue-extension claims until a primary proceedings record confirms them.
