# Privacy Policy

**Version 3.4 · Effective 12 October 2026 · Previous versions at dezbatere.ro/privacy/versions**

**In short.** We keep the language you chose on our site for the emails we send you.

## 13. Cookies and browser storage

Dialectical Engine uses one cookie: `debateai.locale`. The **Cookie Policy** at dezbatere.ro/cookies lists it.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `debateai.locale` | Cookie | Remembers the interface language you picked, and sets the language of the emails we send you. | 1 year |

Each email we queue for you keeps the language you had selected until we send it.

No other party collects information about your online activities over time and across websites through Dialectical Engine.

Browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal or a similar signal. Dialectical Engine does not track you, so there is nothing for such a signal to switch off.

To refuse these items, block or delete cookies and site data for this site in your browser settings.

## 14. Changes to this policy

This policy was last updated on 12 October 2026.
