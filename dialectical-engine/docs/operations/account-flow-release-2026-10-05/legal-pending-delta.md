Passkeys or an authenticator provide the second step; a passkey account does not also have to configure an authenticator. Consumer sessions end after fourteen days of inactivity or thirty days from creation, whichever comes first, subject to earlier revocation.

**Turnstile.** Where enabled on signup and resend forms, Cloudflare Turnstile processes browser security signals to reduce automated abuse. Our Siteverify request contains the challenge token and the server secret, without phone, email, password, birth date or an added IP-address parameter. The browser independently supplies signals to Cloudflare. Cloudflare acts as our processor when protecting the site and as a controller when improving its bot detection. See [Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/), checked 5 October 2026. A five-minute local replay-digest expiry is enforced on access; it is not a promise of physical deletion at that instant or Cloudflare's retention period. Pre-clearance is disabled; no claim about all other Cloudflare cookies follows.

Provider phone/contact data is not requested. An available sign-in button does not itself establish that provider configuration, external access, or local fresh confirmation has succeeded.
