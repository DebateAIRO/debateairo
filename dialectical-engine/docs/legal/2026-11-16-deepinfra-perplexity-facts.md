# DeepInfra and Perplexity: the facts behind their Register rows

Read on 16 November 2026 from the two vendors' public pages. These are the facts
`apps/ui/lib/legal/pages.ts` shows on `/providers` for `DEEPINFRA_PROVIDER`,
`DEEPINFRA_PARTNER_PROVIDER` and `PERPLEXITY_PROVIDER`, and the facts the Privacy Policy (section 5)
and the Terms (section 8) now rely on. It continues `2026-10-10-preview-provider-facts.md`.

These notes are not legal advice. They record what each vendor publishes for every account. Nobody
has compared them with a signed contract or with the settings of our own accounts; the owner (and
counsel, before the public site) confirms them.

### The partner model: Qwen3.8-Flash

Of the four DeepInfra models the preview uses, three run on DeepInfra's own servers and one does not.

| Model | "Partner" tag on its model page | `is_partner` in the page's data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `zai-org/GLM-5.3-Flash` | no | false |
| `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash` | no | false |
| `XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Pro` | no | false |
| `Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash` | yes | true |

- The tag's text: "This model is served by a partner. Your requests are forwarded to them. Their
  privacy policy applies." (https://deepinfra.com/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash)
- The trust centre: partner models "are marked with a 'Partner' tag on their model page, for example
  Anthropic and Google models, Qwen Max models and Qwen3.8-Flash. They run on the partner's
  infrastructure, and the partner's data policy applies." Open-weight models "run on DeepInfra's own
  GPUs. Nothing is sent to DeepSeek, Alibaba, Z.ai, Moonshot, Meta, Google or any other model
  developer".
- Neither page names the partner for Qwen3.8-Flash, its country or its terms.

This answers the question the 10 October note left with the owner ("whether 'Partner' means
DeepInfra forwards Qwen3.8-Flash requests to another company"): it does. So DeepInfra's own facts
(United States only, not stored, no training, standard contractual clauses) do not cover the text
sent to Qwen3.8-Flash. The page gives that company its own row, `DEEPINFRA_PARTNER_PROVIDER`, with
every fact "[to confirm]", as the 10 October note asked: "that company receives debate text and
needs its own row on the page".

**Owner to decide:** ask DeepInfra which company serves Qwen3.8-Flash and under which terms, and then
fill the row; or take Qwen3.8-Flash out of the line-up, and then the row and the sentence about the
partner in the Privacy Policy (section 5) and the Terms (section 8) go.

Decided on 24 November 2026: Qwen3.8-Flash leaves the line-up, and the row and the sentences go; a letter
asking DeepInfra to name the company was drafted. See `2026-11-24-partner-model-out.md`.

The API privacy page adds: "Certain APIs may be subject to product-specific terms". No terms specific
to the Search API were found on 16 November. They were found the next day: the Search Addendum, which
lets Perplexity keep search questions and results and use them to improve its products. The rows
above on retention, zero data retention and training are replaced by
`2026-11-17-provider-contract-facts.md`, and the page and the English texts follow that note.

## What the Privacy Policy and the Terms say after this change

- Both name DeepInfra, say that it receives most of a debate's text, that it runs the models on its
  own servers in the United States, and that it passes one model's requests to a partner company
  under that company's own privacy terms.
- Both say that the terms of DeepInfra, Anthropic and Perplexity do not allow training on the text,
  and that we have not confirmed this for DeepInfra's partner. Before, both said flatly that the text
  "is not used to train models".
- The Privacy Policy says that the Register names the transfer mechanism for each provider and marks
  a provider for which none is confirmed.
