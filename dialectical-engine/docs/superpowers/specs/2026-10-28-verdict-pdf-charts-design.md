# Charts in the verdict PDF — design (2026-10-28)

## Intent

The owner's downloadable report (`GET /debate/{id}/report`) is text only. Where a statement in the story compares
things the debate put numbers on (prices, benchmark scores, the strength of competing positions), a chart drawn right
under that statement makes the comparison visible. The owner's example is a cost-vs-score scatter of AI models
(Artificial Analysis' "Intelligence Index vs. Cost per Task"). Charts appear **only where they make a difference**:
when two measures cover the same items, a scatter; one measure across several items, a bar chart; otherwise nothing.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-28)

- Charts may use **figures the AI models found and cited in their arguments**, labelled as not independently
  verified, **plus the engine's own scores** (strengths).
- The charts are **AI-generated**: an AI model chooses where a chart helps, what it compares and how it is labelled.
- Under every chart: the AI-generated note and **"Copyrighted by DMS Merchandise Shop SRL"** (fixed text, every language).
- The Terms of Service gain a clause that DMS Merchandise Shop SRL owns the charts and statistics it generates.
- A logo goes in the chart's corner once the company has one; until then a text wordmark.

## Calls made by the builder (not decided by the owner)

1. **AI chooses, code draws.** The model never draws; it returns a chart plan as JSON. Code validates every number and
   draws the chart in vector form with react-pdf `<Svg>`. A model cannot add a value the debate never contained.
2. **A separate chart call after the story is accepted** — `STORY:CHARTS:1`, role SYNTHESIZER, story lane, contract
   `story.charts.v1`, sent to the maker that wrote the served story (with the run's cost fallback, as round 1).
   The storyteller and checker contracts stay byte for byte. Any failure (money, parse, provider) means **no charts**;
   the story's outcome never depends on its charts.
3. **Number check.** A `figure` value carries `text` copied from the cited point's claim. Code keeps the chart only when
   that text appears in the claim (whitespace-normalised) and the number it states equals `value` (either decimal
   convention). `strength` values are never taken from the model: the PDF reads the point's final strength (else its
   base strength) from the answer itself, the same numbers the appendix prints. A chart with any failing item is dropped
   whole, never repaired.
4. **Stored once, drawn on every download.** Valid charts ride in the story's sealed content as an optional `charts`
   member (no migration, as R1d's `backup`), are served on `GET /v1/answers/{id}/story` as an optional `charts` field,
   and are owner-only like the long story. Every download shows the same charts; no AI call at download time.
5. **Placement.** A chart is anchored after one paragraph of "Why this answer" (`why.N`) or of the long story
   (`long.S.P`), 1-based. Chart and caption sit in one unbreakable block, kept together on a page.
6. **Limits.** At most 4 charts per story; 2–12 items per chart (scatter: at least 3); labels ≤ 80 characters,
   titles ≤ 160. A log scale only when every value is above zero.
7. **Caption.** Title above the plot; below: "From points P3, P5 and P9" (code-built from the items' references),
   "Chart generated by AI from the debate's arguments.", "Cited figures were not independently verified." (when a
   figure axis is present), "Strengths were calculated by Dialectical Engine." (when a strength axis is present), and
   the copyright line. "About this report" gains one sentence on how the charts were made, only when there are charts.
8. **Corner mark.** `apps/ui/assets/brand/report-logo.png` when the file exists, otherwise the "Dialectical Engine"
   wordmark in the report's muted grey. Dropping the file in is all a logo needs.
9. **Right-to-left reports** mirror the caption alignment and the corner mark; plot coordinates stay left to right.

## Non-goals

- No chart on the public page or the site's story panel (owner-only PDF, like the long story).
- No charts for stories written before this change, and no re-writing of old stories.
- No AI-drawn images, no charts at download time.
- No change to the storyteller's or checker's wording, rounds or money.

## Known limits

- The chart call has no length-guidance step and no thinking-level row of its own: on the private preview it is a
  STANDARD call (`previewCallTierOf`), with the storyteller's bound.
- Right-to-left reports mirror the title row only; the plot's axes run left to right.
- Label widths are estimated (no text measuring before layout): a very long label is cut with an ellipsis.
