never a `minimumReleaseAgeExclude` entry, so no exclusion was added and the sync waited.

unchanged. A diagnostic resolve with vitest held at 4.1.11 had already established that
the vitest family was the *whole* block: everything else resolved, 478 packages.

**Do not "fix" this by excluding a version.** The cooldown is worthless the moment it has
an escape hatch, and ten hours is the entire point of it.
