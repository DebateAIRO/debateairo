## 4. New findings (regressions / residues introduced or exposed by rework)

### D1. Major — “Every clause tagged” is false for requirements body

**Severity:** Major  
**Where:** `requirements-spec.md` §2.1 claim vs ~143 untagged `Requirement` heads; contrast §26 discipline  
**Evidence:** §2.1 asserts three tags on every normative clause. Sample untagged imperatives: F-1…F-8, A-5, A-6, S-1 steps 1–4 (“recompose or defect flag”), C-6/C-7, most D-/E- rows. Tagged islands (F-10…, C-5a, S-2…, N-9…) show the intended pattern was applied only to rework hotspots.  
**Why it bites:** A builder cannot mechanically distinguish RULED / CARRIED-DESIGN / CANDIDATE without re-deriving DRs — the exact failure the authority rework existed to end.  
**Concrete fix:** Either (a) tag every requirement head, defaulting DR-entailed text to `RULED(DR-n)` or `[CD]`, or (b) demote §2.1 to “every clause that answers an open OD, plus every reworked coexistence clause” and list the untagged corpus as “implicitly RULED by §3 disposition unless marked.”  
**Refutation attempted:** “Untagged means carried-from-DR batch.” Insufficient — §2.1’s words are universal; R-AUTH-2 is unenforceable without tags.  
**Proof that flips:** Grep-clean: every `**Requirement` carries one of the three tags, or §2.1 is rewritten to a weaker, true claim.
