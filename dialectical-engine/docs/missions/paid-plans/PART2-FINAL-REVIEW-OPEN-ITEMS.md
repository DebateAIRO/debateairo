| P2-M10 (owner, accountant) | What W5 closed: a retry of a failed renewal payment charges exactly the total of the period's latest priced attempt. Its quote is copied and the tax service is not asked, so no retry charges an amount the person was not told. When no attempt was priced (Q-1: the tax service was down for the renewal's 72 hours), the retry is priced afresh and charged only if the fresh total equals the announced one. Any other total is a failed attempt with no charge (`RETRY_TOTAL_CHANGED`) on the normal retry dates, which ends in ENDED(DUNNING), Free and M6 (spec A29 (g)). Two decisions are still open. (1) The owner either keeps that fallback or rules LC-3's first option: send M3 (the price-change notice) from PAST_DUE, then wait 7 business days before charging the new total, with the grace lengthened to match. The subscription history already accepts RENEWAL_NOTICE_SENT on PAST_DUE; `writeNotice` does not, so that option is a code change. Keeping the fallback costs this: a person whose card works loses the plan and can subscribe again only at the new price. Also, each card change brings the next no-charge attempt forward (a card change triggers an immediate retry), so the plan can end days early. (2) Q-1's "no charge without a fresh quote" now covers only the renewal's quiet 72 hours: a retry of a priced attempt is charged on that attempt's quote even while the tax service is down. The accountant (with go-live row 16) confirms the tax point. A retry is invoiced on its own payment date (Quaderno's `date` and SmartBill's `issueDate` are the payment's time), but at the rate, tax status and amounts of the quote made when the period started. EU VAT on a payment made in advance generally becomes due when the payment is received, at the rate in force then, so after a rate change during the retries the old rate may be wrong. If the accountant says the payment-date rate applies, such a retry cannot charge the old total: it then takes the `RETRY_TOTAL_CHANGED` path (a fresh quote on every retry again, so a tax-service outage fails the retry again) or follows the owner's notice ruling.
