# Israel: what must be in place before the first Israeli account

Date: 1 October 2026. Not reviewed by counsel. Sources: Protection of Privacy Law 5741-1981 as amended by
Amendment 13 (in force 14 August 2025), and the Protection of Privacy (Data Security) Regulations 5777-2017, read
in the consolidated Hebrew text on Hebrew Wikisource with gazette references (gov.il was behind a bot check), so
treat the wording as [unverified] against the official gazette.

| Duty | Applies to us? | State |
|---|---|---|
| Register the database (s.8A(a)) | No: only data-broker databases over 10,000 people and public bodies | — |
| Notify the PPA within 30 days once "specially sensitive" data covers more than 100,000 people (s.8A(b)) | Not at launch; political and religious views count | Watch the number |
| Privacy protection officer (s.17B1) | Not at launch; para. (4) catches a controller whose main business includes specially sensitive data "at significant scale" | Review as Israeli use grows |
| Security officer (s.17B) | No: only owners of five registrable databases, banks, public bodies | — |
| Data-collection notice (s.11, incl. new 2a: controller's name and contact) | Yes | **Done** in Privacy Policy B.9 (1 October 2026): it now names DebateAIRO S.R.L. and points to section 1 |
| Data Security Regulations: our database is **medium** level (political or religious views, First Schedule 1(3)(d)); **basic** if no more than 10 people can reach it | Yes | **Open:** write the database definitions document and the security procedure, and keep an incident log (regs 2, 4, 11(a)) [regs 2–10 not read in detail] |
| Report a "severe security incident" to the PPA "immediately" (reg. 11(d)) | Only at medium or high level | See `breach-clocks.md` |

The Law has no territorial clause; assume it reaches us because we serve people in Israel.
