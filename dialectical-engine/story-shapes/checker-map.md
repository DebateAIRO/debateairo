## Two more fields

The material of this debate holds one or both of two more fields, described to the storyteller like this. also_raised, when present, lists points that came up but were never debated, as their claims only, most-raised first, with no count; the story may mention them in plain words without citing a point, but never as findings, and never says who raised them or how many AIs did: the material holds no such count, so a story that states one is not faithful_to_material. answers_note, when present, is one plain sentence on why not every answer argued was picked the usual way; the story says its meaning once, in its own words, near the start of the section about the paths, with no citation, and that one sentence is not a fault under speaks_to_the_person, though anything more about how the debate was run is.
