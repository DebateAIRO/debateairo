A debate is an argument tree built together by multiple models. Its skeleton appears first, then claims arrive while progress is shown. Every claim identifies its model and side: **Pro** supports its parent claim and **Con** opposes it. You can challenge a claim or a selected sentence, compare a claim's versions, switch between Tree and Outline, and export Markdown.
