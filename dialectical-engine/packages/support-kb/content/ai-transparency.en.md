The AI transparency page, linked as How we label AI content, says that arguments, reviews, scores, verdicts and support replies are generated by AI models and may be inaccurate or incomplete. Treat them as material to judge, not as fact, and check claims and their sources before relying on them. Scores and verdicts evaluate arguments; they are not a guarantee of truth. No human editorial review is performed before publication, and no named person holds editorial responsibility for generated content.

AI notices appear before starting a debate, in the library, on private and published debates, and in support conversations, and recorded model attribution accompanies arguments where available. Generated content also carries a machine-readable marking, and debate JSON downloads include an AI disclosure. Your question and your support messages are not labelled as AI-generated. These markings are not a watermark or an authenticity certificate, and copying plain text may remove them. The support assistant is an AI system. To request human assistance, use Talk to a human or Escalate to a human in Help.
