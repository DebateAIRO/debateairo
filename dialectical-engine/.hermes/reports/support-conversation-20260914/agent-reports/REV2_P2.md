## Cause and price

The correction fixed the author examples but two safety mechanisms still define security by enumerating surface forms. Supplied-value detection recognizes a closing quote, at most six whitespace-delimited words, and a narrow punctuation grammar. When a recognized credential label is followed by an unmatched quote, a seventh word, or an embedded period, the analyzer either returns no span or returns a prefix span; every downstream consumer then faithfully persists and transits the residual. The remedy is a fail-closed span invariant after a credential label and connector, not another increase in the word limit.

Operation negation has the same structural problem. A regular expression enumerates coordinators and selected next tokens. New verb groups beginning with `may`, Romanian `poate`, or `plus` stay in the earlier negated scope, so a later positive receive operation is marked negated. The price is another P1 rework pass even though the exact author strings and 381 focused tests passed. It also makes every future wording variant a manual corpus-maintenance problem.

The canonical-view helper does terminate and fail closed at exhaustion, but its consumer does not recognize a successfully decoded backslash path. `%5Csettings` therefore passes after canonicalization. The earlier max-pass-only mutant was correctly labeled non-discriminating: it tested the loop bound while an independent exhaustion guard kept the tested cases safe, and could not prove the downstream path grammar complete.

The bounded run took one actual-module invocation and no service traffic. Mechanical custody checked 103 product files, 82 GATE immutable inputs, and 26 compact lens inputs with zero mismatches. The probe found three open safety classes plus a compatibility regression, so another author/reviewer cycle is required. The current actual matrix remains 3/7 useful with four refusals, but the discarded completion text prevents pricing any individual synthetic false positive as the cause of a live refusal.
