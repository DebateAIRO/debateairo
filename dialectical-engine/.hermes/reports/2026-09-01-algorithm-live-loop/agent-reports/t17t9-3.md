## 6d. B1 — what the 19 unhandled `AUTH_MAIL_BUSY` rejections actually are

Full record: `logs/t17t9-3/24-b1-experiment-summary.txt`, runs `21`–`23`.

### The mechanism, read from the source rather than inferred from the log

- `apps/api/src/registration.ts:1073` — `waitDeadlineMs = channel.mailDispatchQueueWaitTimeoutMs`,
  which is **18 000 ms** (`packages/register/src/auth-policy.ts:778`). A **real wall-clock timer**.
- `registration.ts:1086-1096` — a queued dispatch that reaches that deadline splices itself out of
  `waitingMailDispatches` and **rejects** with `AUTH_MAIL_BUSY`. This is the line codex named (`:1094`).
- `registration-database.test.ts` `runDeepRoute` — `targetCalls` / `markerCalls` are pushed and only
  awaited at `await Promise.all(targetCalls)`, **after** several `waitFor` gates. A waiter that
  rejects before that line rejects with nobody attached → **unhandled**. The same expiry empties the
  queue and starves the grant records the gate is polling for → **`S3D_R3_WAIT_TIMEOUT`**.

**Round 2 had the causal direction backwards.** I assumed the test failure abandoned the promises.
