3% conservative**, NOT the 94-versus-109 "16% loose" I reported. I derived it independently before

with its own criteria AND a non-empty objection, so the rejecting verdict carries both — and accepts
on round 3. It **throws** `T17_EVALUATOR_ROUND_UNREADABLE` rather than defaulting, and the fixture's
sealed `evaluatorLoopMaxRounds` and the double's acceptance round are now ONE constant, so they
